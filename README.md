#### Description: 

The core structure and navigation of the website are built around `index.html` and `footer.html`. `index.html` serves as the main landing page, introducing visitors to **The Advocates Rise**, showcasing the band's identity, and acting as the central hub that links to every other section of the site. Working alongside it, `footer.html` provides a consistent, reusable bottom navigation and information bar across pages, ensuring visitors have easy access to social links, copyright details, and quick navigation no matter where they are on the site.

To highlight the band's catalog and live shows, the site includes dedicated media and event pages in `music.html` and `tour.html`. `music.html` is designed as the audio and discography showcase, allowing listeners to explore releases, stream tracks, and learn more about the band's sound. Meanwhile, `tour.html` keeps fans updated on upcoming performances, featuring dates, venues, and ticket or event details so followers can catch the band live.

Finally, community engagement, merchandise, and direct communication are handled through `fanclub.html`, `shop.html`, and `contact.html`. `fanclub.html` gives dedicated supporters a space to sign up for exclusive updates and connect more closely with the band, while `shop.html` acts as the official merch storefront for browsing apparel and gear. Rounding out the site, `contact.html` provides a straightforward way for fans, venues, and press to get in touch for bookings, inquiries, and general messages.
